ResearchWhy lead-free perovskites

The best perovskite solar cells still contain lead, and that is a big reason they are not on rooftops yet. My PhD is about the version without it.

Tin-based perovskites are safer, but they still trail lead-based cells on efficiency and stability. I work on closing that gap across the whole chain: making the films and devices, measuring them, and modeling what happens inside. I came to solar cells from semiconductor device physics, so I look at a cell as a device first: interfaces, trap states and band alignment.

Fabrication

Tin-based perovskite films and solar cells processed in an inert-atmosphere glovebox, with spin coating and thermal evaporation.

Characterization

FESEM imaging of film morphology, XRD for crystal structure and phase, UV-Vis spectroscopy for optical properties.

Device modeling

Simulation of band alignment, trap states and interfaces, building on three papers of Sentaurus TCAD work.
